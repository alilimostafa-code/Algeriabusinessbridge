About

Expertise built at the crossroads of several disciplines

Our firm brings together two senior consultants whose complementary backgrounds span project engineering, investment, governance and business management.

Senior consultant

Alili Mostafa

Senior investment consultant, author and arts researcher

His professional path was built at the crossroads of rural engineering, agronomy, investment, computer science and territorial development. For more than fifteen years, he has supported project developers, farmers, companies and institutions in the design, analysis and implementation of development and investment projects.

A graduate in forestry and soil science, with an additional degree in computer science, he has developed an approach based on the complementarity of technical skills, economic analysis, digital tools and project management.

Since 2011, he has worked as a consultant and manager of a consulting firm specialising in rural engineering, agricultural projects and investment. His work includes conducting techno-economic studies, supporting investment projects, analysing and designing rural development solutions, sizing technical networks and facilities, and developing computer tools for calculation, simulation, modelling, management and decision support.

Throughout his career, he has also worked with various public institutions, professional bodies, farms and private companies, giving him a practical understanding of the issues surrounding agricultural development, investment, rural development and project implementation across different regional contexts.

Alongside his consulting work, he is also the author of several technical, professional, historical and philosophical books. His publications on investment, consulting and business development extend his professional experience.

Today, he puts this experience and multidisciplinary vision at the service of investors, companies, institutions and project developers seeking serious analysis, independent expertise and a comprehensive approach to turn an idea or opportunity into a structured, coherent and achievable project.

Senior consultant

Ali Mouas

Senior consultant — Governance, management and business development

Senior consultant with solid experience in corporate governance, management and operations. His career has notably included senior executive roles — director and chief executive officer — within major Algerian state-owned enterprises (EPE), where he developed an in-depth understanding of management, governance and organisational steering.

This experience gave him proven expertise in understanding the institutional and economic environment of companies, organisational management, activity coordination and support for decision-making processes. He also has practical knowledge of the challenges related to performance, internal organisation and the development of professional structures.

A member of the Chamber of Agriculture of the wilaya of Algiers, Ali Mouas also has valuable experience and knowledge of the agricultural and institutional environment, as well as the stakeholders and issues related to the development of the sector.

Thanks to his high-level experience, his understanding of the institutional world, and his command of the challenges related to the management and development of organisations, Ali Mouas is a reference partner for assignments requiring expertise in governance, management, organisation and corporate strategy.
