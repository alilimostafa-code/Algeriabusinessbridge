Concrete solutions to turn your projects into opportunities

Strategic consulting & studies

  • Market studies and sector analysis
  • Opportunity and feasibility studies
  • Economic and strategic analysis
  • Company and project diagnostics
  • Techno-economic studies
  • Economic intelligence and monitoring

Goal: turn information into strategic decisions.

02

Project engineering & investment

From the initial idea to project structuring, consistent support adapted to the Algerian context.

  • Identification and analysis of investment opportunities
  • Project design and structuring
  • Techno-economic and financial studies
  • Business plan development

Goal: turn an idea into a structured, achievable project.

Goal: ease your market entry and reduce risk.

Business development & growth
