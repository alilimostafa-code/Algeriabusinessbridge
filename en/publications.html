Publications & books

Expertise grounded in research and publishing

Throughout his career, Alili Mostafa has built in-depth expertise in investment, rural and entrepreneurial development, the environment and business consulting — expertise reflected in several reference books aimed at investors, entrepreneurs, consultants and engineering firms.

The comprehensive guide to investment in Algeria

Legal, fiscal, financial and strategic framework — Independent edition 2025–2026

Algeria is entering a new economic era, driven by deep reforms and a clear ambition to open up to investors. Far more than a simple guide, this reference work on current Algerian law is designed to support every stage of an investment project.

Legal and institutional framework · tax regime and incentives · financing solutions · promising sectors · practical steps for setting up and sustaining a business.

The profession of investment and business-development consultant

Professional handbook, firm-creation guide and skills framework — 2026 edition

From setting up a consulting firm to running high-value assignments, this book offers a complete methodology for practising as an investment and business-development consultant.

Professional practices · operational tools · case studies · skills frameworks — an essential guide for consultants, entrepreneurs and decision-makers.
