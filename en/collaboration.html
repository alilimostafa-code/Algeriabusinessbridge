Italy–Algeria business meeting: our firm at the heart of economic exchanges

Algiers, 28 September 2026

The El Aurassi hotel in Algiers hosted a major business meeting bringing together Italian and Algerian economic stakeholders, organised in continuation of the Italy–Algeria Business Forum 2025.

The meeting was opened by Mr Maurizio Motta, representative of ICE — the Algiers Agency, followed by welcoming remarks from H.E. Ms Alessandra Schiavo, Ambassador of Italy to Algeria.

Discussions covered several strategic areas of economic cooperation, including the mechanisation and digitalisation of agriculture, export opportunities, Italian agricultural equipment and technologies, as well as investment and project-development prospects in Timimoun.

An active presence for our firm

Our firm took part in this major business meeting, which provides a valuable platform for strengthening business relations between Italian and Algerian companies.

On this occasion, we had the opportunity to engage directly with the entire Italian business delegation, as well as with several Algerian companies and economic operators present at the event.

These exchanges made it possible to explore various opportunities for partnership, commercial cooperation, investment and project support in Algeria, particularly in agriculture, technology, equipment and territorial development.

Our participation is fully in line with our approach of connecting international economic players with business opportunities in Algeria, helping them understand the local economic environment, and supporting companies in their market-entry, development and partnership projects.

B2B exchanges geared towards new opportunities

After a working lunch, participants took part in a series of B2B meetings, encouraging direct exchanges between companies and helping identify complementarities, potential projects and new avenues for cooperation.

The meeting concluded on a particularly positive note, reflecting growing interest in developing lasting, mutually beneficial economic partnerships between Italy and Algeria.

For our firm, this participation represents a further opportunity to grow its network of international partners, strengthen its role as an interface between economic operators, and help build bridges between international expertise and investment opportunities in Algeria.

A day rich in exchanges, encounters and prospects for cooperation, marked by economic dialogue and closer ties between Italy and Algeria.
